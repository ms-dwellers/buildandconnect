# Polish audit, November 2026

A code and UI/UX audit of every route and component, gathered after the white
canvas, social sign-in, and Three.js landing page landed on `feat/polish-sprint`.
Findings are grouped by area and ranked within each group. Severity means:

- **Blocker**: breaks a flow, misleads the user, or contradicts a stated rule in a
  way visitors will notice.
- **Should fix**: visible clunkiness or inconsistency that undermines the polished
  feel.
- **Nice to have**: small cleanups and code hygiene.

Every finding below was confirmed in the source. Items marked _browser_ also
need a visual pass in the browser before or during their batch, since this audit
was gathered from code and server-rendered HTML.

## Proposed fix batches

| Batch | Area                              | Findings           |
| ----- | --------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| A     | Tokens, shared chrome, navigation | T1 to T5, C1 to C4 |

## Tokens and colour (Batch A)

**T1. Muted text is indistinguishable from body text.** Should fix.
`--muted-foreground: #254139` against `--foreground: #123d37` is 1.08:1, so
"muted" copy carries no hierarchy anywhere (leads, descriptions, captions).
Fix: a visibly lighter muted foreground that still clears 4.5:1 on white and on
mint, for example a grey-green around `#4b6861`, verified by
`tests/theme.test.ts`.

**T2. Three near-identical mints.** Should fix. `--secondary #cce0d9`,
`--muted #c0d7ce`, and `--accent #a9c8bd` differ by 1.1 to 1.3:1, so secondary
buttons, muted surfaces, and hover accents all read as the same tint. Fix: keep
`--secondary` as the one mint surface, make `--muted` a neutral light surface
for skeletons and quiet panels, and give `--accent` enough contrast to register
as a hover state.

**T3. Status colours collapse into the primary.** Blocker for dashboards.
`--success`, `--info`, `--info-foreground`, and `--success-foreground` all equal
`--palette-pine` (`app/globals.css`), so "Open" and "Accepted"
(`components/dashboards/parts.tsx`) and "Connected" versus "The requester is
interested" (`components/opportunity-detail.tsx`) look identical.
Fix: success on emerald `#1e6348` with a pale emerald surface, info on a dark
teal, each with its own foreground, and keep warning on apricot.

**T4. Mismatched edges and borders.** Should fix. `--button-secondary-edge:
#99713b` is brown under a mint secondary button (map filters in
`components/map/calgary-explorer.tsx`, volunteer markers in
`components/map/job-marker.tsx`, debug toggles). `--border` at pine 20% is
1.4:1 on white while `--input: #3e5c53` is 6.8:1, so cards and dividers vanish
while inputs, selects, and outline buttons look heavy. Fix: derive the secondary
edge from the mint family (a teal-dark), raise `--border` to roughly 30% pine,
and soften `--input` toward the border weight.

**T5. Control heights disagree in the same row.** Should fix, _browser_.
`Button` is `h-10` plus a 3px edge; `Input` is `sm:h-7.5` and `Select`
`sm:min-h-8` (`components/ui/input.tsx`, `components/ui/select.tsx`). Visible in
`components/MapPinPicker.tsx` (search beside Search), `components/profile-editor.tsx`
(skill input, level select, icon button), and
`components/forms/opportunity-form.tsx` (place name beside Remove pin). Fix:
align the input and select heights with the default button on the `sm`
breakpoint, in the primitives, once.

## Shared chrome and navigation (Batch A)

**C1. Every navigation is a full page reload.** Should fix. 26 plain internal
`<a href="/...">` links in `app/` and `components/` against a single `next/link`
use (`components/map/map-journey.tsx`). Every click re-downloads the app and
re-runs the session fetch. Fix: `Link` for all internal navigation, keeping the
raw anchors only where a full reload is intended.

**C2. Pages flicker while the session loads.** Should fix, _browser_.
`lib/session.tsx` starts in `loading`, so `AppHeader`
(`components/app/app-shell.tsx`) shows the guest nav and then swaps to the role
nav, and `app/dashboard/page.tsx` retitles from "Dashboard" to "Developer
dashboard" after load. Fix: render session-neutral chrome (no sign-in or
dashboard controls) until the session resolves, and give the dashboard a
stable title.

**C3. Five different page headers.** Should fix. The landing header, `AppHeader`,
the debug console, the design-system gallery (sticky with `backdrop-blur-sm`,
against the no-gloss rule), and the voice prototype each build their own. Fix:
one `AppHeader` with a `tone` prop for the landing variant; drop the blur.

**C4. Raised buttons inside navigation, and badges as labels.** Should fix.
"Get started" in `AppHeader` and "Your dashboard" in
`components/app/account-links.tsx` are raised outline buttons inside nav, where
AGENTS.md asks for flat links. Badges mark ordinary labels: the city in
`components/dashboards/developer-dashboard.tsx`, "QA console" in
`app/debug/console.tsx`, "Local demo" in `app/wip-voice-ai/voice-studio.tsx`, and
a success badge for "Paid project" in `components/map/job-marker.tsx`. Fix: flat
nav links with one primary action; plain text for labels; status colours only
for status.

## Auth and onboarding (Batch B)

**O1. The permanent role choice commits on one click.** Blocker. `app/role/page.tsx`
tells people the choice cannot be changed, then commits on a single click with no
confirmation. Fix: select a card, show a short summary of what that role can do,
and confirm with a button; keep the 409 handling.

**O2. The destination is lost through sign-up.** Should fix. `AuthForm` now
carries `?next` between sign-in and sign-up, but sign-up always lands on `/role`
and the role page ignores `next`, so a visitor who clicked "Post a request" ends
on the dashboard. Fix: pass `next` through `/role` and honour it after the role
is set.

**O3. "Post a request" sends the wrong roles to a dead end.** Should fix. The home
page shows it to everyone; developers reach "This page is for Requester
accounts" (`components/app/require-role.tsx`). Fix: route
signed-in non-requesters to their dashboard with an inline explanation, and send
guests through sign-up with `next` set.

**O4. Network failures leave buttons spinning.** Should fix. `api()` in
`lib/api.ts` never catches a failed `fetch`, so a dropped connection leaves
`AuthForm`, `RequestForm`, `OpportunityForm`, `ProfileForm`, `AreaForm`, the
role picker, `useMutation`, and `useApi` stuck in their loading state. Fix:
catch in `api()` and return `{ ok: false, status: 0, error: "You appear to be
offline" }`.

**O5. Role cards are hand-built.** Nice to have. `app/role/page.tsx` styles
native buttons as cards instead of composing `Card`. Fix: `Card` with a selected
state, which also serves O1.

## Dashboards and forms (Batch C)

**D1. The developer profile is one long column with three save buttons.**
Should fix, _browser_. `components/profile-editor.tsx` (535 lines) stacks roughly
twenty inputs plus repeating skill and project rows, two cards disabled until the
first save, and separate saves for profile, preferences, and area. The
availability and discoverability switches also exist on the dashboard with
instant saves, so the same setting saves two different ways. Fix: sections with
one save, a clear first-run path, and one home for the two switches.

**D2. Inconsistent post-submit behaviour.** Should fix. A new opportunity goes to
its detail page with a toast; a new request goes to the dashboard. Analysis runs
with no progress text or timeout. Fix: both land on the created record, with a
"Analysing with AI" state and a timeout message.

**D3. Recruiter dashboard is a read-only list.** No longer applies. Main has
since reduced accounts to developers and requesters, so the recruiter dashboard
is gone; nothing to fix.

**D4. Requester dashboard has two competing primary buttons.** Should fix. "Post an
opportunity" in the header and "Describe a project with voice" in the body both
read as primary. Fix: one primary (post), voice intake as a secondary link.

**D5. Hand-built toggle groups and inline alerts.** Should fix. Map filters
(`calgary-explorer.tsx`) and the debug console build toggles from `Button` plus
`aria-pressed` instead of `toggle-group` or `segmented-control`; twelve inline
`<p role="alert">` patterns sit beside `ErrorAlert`; `ChoiceGroup` lives in
`opportunity-form.tsx` but is imported by two other forms. Fix: adopt the coss
primitives, move `ChoiceGroup` into `components/forms/form-kit.tsx`, and route
errors through `ErrorAlert` or `FieldError`.

**D6. Font rules make section headings mono.** Should fix. `label, legend {
font-family: DM Mono }` in `app/globals.css` turns `FormSection` legends
(`components/forms/form-kit.tsx`) and switch labels into faux-bold mono (only
weights 400 and 500 ship), while group labels rendered as `<p>` stay sans. Space
Mono is loaded but unused. Fix: mono for field labels only, legends in League
Spartan, drop Space Mono and its test expectation.

**D7. Copy inconsistencies.** Nice to have. "hrs / week" versus "hours/week",
"Experience level 3 of 5" versus "3 · Capable", "Calgary, AB" versus "Calgary,
Alberta", filter labels "Paid" versus badge "Paid project". Fix: a small
`lib/format.ts` for hours and levels, and the shared `CITY_NAME` for the city.

## Public map (Batch D)

**M1. The map overlay card is hand-styled with a heavy shadow.** Should fix.
`components/map/calgary-explorer.tsx` uses `shadow-lg` on a hand-built card;
`components/map/calgary-canvas.tsx` styles three more overlays by hand. Fix:
`Card` with `shadow-card`.

**M2. List items hide detail from screen readers.** Should fix. Each list entry
is a ghost button with `aria-label="View {title}"`, which replaces the visible
neighbourhood and pay text for assistive technology. Fix: drop the override and
let the visible content be the name, with "View" as visually hidden suffix.

**M3. Volunteer and community listings share one colour and legend key.** Should
fix. Both kinds render mint and both legend keys use `map-key-volunteer`
(`calgary-canvas.tsx`, `lib/calgary-map.ts`). Fix: distinct tints from the token
set and matching legend keys.

**M4. Two Mapbox styles.** Nice to have. `/map` uses the `standard` style while
`components/MapPinPicker.tsx` uses `streets-v12`, so the request form's map looks
unrelated. Fix: one style constant.

**M5. Requester copy for a missing token.** Should fix. Without a Mapbox token
the request form tells visitors to set `NEXT_PUBLIC_MAPBOX_ACCESS_TOKEN` in
`.env.local`. Fix: a plain "Map unavailable right now" message with the
developer hint only in development.

## Hire and voice intake (Batch E)

**H1. `/hire` runs a second colour scheme.** Blocker. `.voice-studio` in
`app/globals.css` overrides `--primary` to olive and `app/hire/page.tsx` imports
`app/wip-voice-ai/voice.css` with rust and moss gradients, so one page shows
olive and pine buttons together and breaks the no-gradient rule.
`components/hiring/employer-intake.tsx` imports `Aurora`, `Waveform`, and
`useMicrophone` from the prototype folder. Fix: render the hire flow on the main
tokens, move the three shared pieces into `components/hiring/`, and leave the
prototype's palette inside `/wip-voice-ai` only.

**H2. Thirteen fields in one fieldset.** Should fix. `components/hiring/brief-form.tsx`
has no sections. Fix: `FormSection` groups mirroring the opportunity form.

**H3. Vendor names in user-facing copy.** Should fix. "Send to Gemini",
OpenRouter, and ElevenLabs appear in `employer-intake.tsx`. Fix: "Draft the
brief" and "Transcribe", with providers named only in docs.

**H4. The results screen is a dead end.** Should fix. Suggested developers end
with "Use your dashboard to post an opportunity". Fix: a "Post this as an
opportunity" button that prefills the opportunity form from the brief.

## Code quality, no UI change (Batch F)

**Q1. `Role` is defined four times.** Should fix. `backend/schemas.ts`,
`lib/api.ts`, `convex/schema.ts`, and a zod enum in `backend/api.ts`. Fix: one
`ROLES` tuple in `backend/schemas.ts` that derives the type, the zod enum, and
the Convex validator.

**Q2. Client components import runtime code from `backend/`.** No longer applies:
AGENTS.md now defines `backend/` as shared pure validation and matching helpers,
so client code may import them. Original note:
`components/developer-preferences-form.tsx` and
`components/hiring/brief-form.tsx` import values, not types, from `@/backend/*`,
against the note in `lib/types.ts`. Fix: move the shared constants to `lib/`.

**Q3. No `not-found.tsx`, `error.tsx`, or `loading.tsx`.** Should fix. Unknown
routes and thrown errors fall through to framework defaults. Fix: the three
files with the app's chrome.

**Q4. Titles are inconsistent.** Nice to have. Separators `|`, `·`, and `—` are
all in use and seven pages have no title of their own. Fix: a `pageTitle()`
helper and metadata on every page.

**Q5. Oversized files.** Nice to have. `app/debug/console.tsx` (825 lines),
`convex/data.ts` (799), `app/wip-voice-ai/voice.css` (681),
`app/wip-voice-ai/voice-studio.tsx` (581), `components/hiring/employer-intake.tsx`
(428). Fix: split the console into session, request editor, and response panels;
split `convex/data.ts` by domain.

**Q6. The debug console is out of date.** Should fix. Its role picker omits
`requester` and it lacks presets for `/requests*`, `/map`, `/hiring/*`,
`/developers/me/location`, `/developers/me/preferences`, and
`/opportunities/mine`. Fix: presets for every route and both roles, with a
test that compares preset paths to the Convex HTTP routes.

**Q7. Dead tokens and types.** Nice to have. Eight `--sidebar-*` tokens and
`--map-water-edge` are unused; `SessionUser` in `lib/api.ts` is unused. Fix:
remove them.

**Q8. Accessibility details.** Should fix. `aria-label` on plain `div`s
(`components/app/require-role.tsx`, `components/MapPinPicker.tsx`,
`components/map/calgary-canvas.tsx`), radio groups labelled by a `<p>` instead of
`fieldset` and `legend`, always-present empty `role="alert"` elements in the
hiring and debug screens, and the apricot warning icon at 1.9:1 on white. Fix:
`role="status"` or real landmarks, fieldsets, conditional alerts, and a darker
warning icon colour.

## Ported to main, November 8

The sprint branch was rebuilt on `main` after it moved the application API and
AI execution into Convex, reduced accounts to developers and requesters, and
added the live requester voice interviews. The port kept every landing, theme,
journey, and sign-in change; social sign-in now reports provider availability
from the Convex HTTP action and the Worker stays a forwarder. Batch 2 was
reconciled with main: the brief-to-opportunity handoff uses main's in-memory
draft context instead of browser storage, the hire intake keeps main's live
flow and loses only the prototype styling and vendor names, and D3 is dropped
with the recruiter role. Five tests that were already failing on main (stale
hiring Worker bindings, an extensionless generated import, and company sign-ups
in the Convex HTTP tests) were aligned so `bun run check` is green again.
